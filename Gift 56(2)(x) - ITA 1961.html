Special Assessment Rules

Crucial legal provisions for timeline mismatches and asset tracking definitions.

DOA

Date of Agreement vs. Date of Registration

Where Date of Agreement (DOA) ≠ Date of Registration (DOR), the Stamp Duty Value (SDV) as on the DOA can be adopted.

Condition: Only if consideration or part thereof is paid on or before the DOA by way of 4 eligible electronic/banking modes.
ASSET

Asset Nature Limitation Rule

Provisions of Section 56(2)(x) apply only when the property received is in the nature of a Capital Asset () in the hands of the recipient.

Does NOT apply if held as Stock-in-Trade (SIT) ()

Receipts from these specific sources or occasions are fully protected from gift tax exposure.

  • (i) From any Relative
  • (ii) On the occasion of Marriage
  • (iii) Under a Will / Inheritance
  • (iv) In contemplation of Death

  • (v) From a Local Authority u/s 10(20)

  • (x) Other prescribed

Statutory Meanings Defined

Strict interpretations defined directly within the boundaries of Section 56(2).

Meaning of "Relative"

An exhaustive relational lineage coverage map mapped out under the Act:

Individual (Ind) Center Reference

Includes Spouse, Brothers (Bro) and Sisters (Sis) of both the individual and the spouse. Lineal ascendants or descendants of both individual and spouse.

For HUF (Hindu Undivided Family): All Members

Meaning of "Property"

Limited scope property definitions. Only the items listed below generate a taxable event:

Capital Asset (Stock in Trade )

  • Land & Building (B/D)
  • Shares / Securities
  • [DJASAP] Bullion, Art, etc.
  • VDA (Virtual Digital Assets / Crypto)
Explicit Exclusions:
(i) Car Not Covered
(ii) Agricultural Land Not Covered

Meaning of "Family"

As applicable for tracing COVID-19 relief exclusions and dependencies:

Spouse & Children
Dependent Category:

Parents, Brothers, and Sisters or any of them, provided they are wholly/mainly dependent on the individual.

Deals with the explicit tax treatment of Bonus Shares received by shareholders. The ruling affirms that the receipt of bonus shares does not attract provisions of Section 56(2)(x).

Section 49: Cost of Acquisition (COA) Alignment

If an Immovable Property (IP) has already been subject to tax assessment under Section 56(2)(x) at the time of receipt, a corresponding adjustments mechanism applies upon its future sale:

Calculation Adjustment Formulation

COA = Value assessed u/s 56(2)(x)

Prevents double taxation on the value element already taxed as a gift.
