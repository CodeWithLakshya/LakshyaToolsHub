Documentation & Guide

Methodology: Product Method

Calculates interest based on the Daily Product Method, ideal for fluctuating capital accounts.

Total Interest = (Sum of Products × Rate) / Days in Year
  • Product: Daily Balance × Number of days balance was held.
  • Leap Year: Auto-detects Feb 29th and uses 366 days if applicable.

Key Functionalities

  • Bulk Import: Upload transactions via Excel. System automatically skips duplicates to prevent data inflation.
  • Smart Export: Generate individual partner reports or a combined firm-wide workbook.
  • Live Balancing: Updates running balances and interest calculations in real-time.
