📌 Documentation & Policy Rules

Penalty Tiers:

  • 0 - 6 Months: No Interest.
  • 6 Months - 1 Year: ROI minus 3% penalty.
  • 1 Year & Above: ROI minus 2% penalty.

Calculation Logic:

TDS: Applied on the earned interest before final payout.

Interest Deducted: Shows loss due to premature penalty (Original ROI vs Effective ROI) for the days held.
