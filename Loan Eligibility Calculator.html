Read Me: How it Works

  • FOIR: Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio. It is the percentage of your income that banks believe you can safely commit to debt repayments.
  • Formula: Eligible EMI = (Total Income × FOIR%) - Existing EMIs.
  • Loan Limit: The maximum loan is calculated using the reducing balance interest formula based on your eligible EMI, tenure, and interest rate.
